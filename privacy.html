Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

The Sto. Niño Devotional App is an independent devotional service operated by Bienuel C. Tenio. Contact: contact@crownmind.net.

Account required

Sign in to use the devotional features. The browser stores reading preferences and may keep a local copy of devotional data associated with your account.

Previous GCash checkout

GCash checkout is currently paused. Candles and flowers can be offered for free.

GCash processes the ₱5 Express Send transfer. The operator compares your submitted transaction reference with the transfer received in GCash. The checkout stores a digest of the reference, an order identifier, the offering type and the review status. Prayer notes stay out of the payment request.

Accounts

The app uses Supabase authentication for email and password management. The service stores account identifiers, private prayer intentions, candle metadata and novena progress in a database restricted by per-user access rules. The account provider manages authentication data. Do not enter highly sensitive information you do not wish to store.

Use and sharing

Information is used only to display and synchronize requested devotional features. The initial release does not include a public prayer wall, advertising, paid spiritual services or sale of personal data. Operational hosting providers may process technical service data according to their own policies.

Retention and choices

You may export your devotional data or delete all your candles and flowers under Settings & Privacy. Signed-in users can delete their Sto. Niño Devotional profile and devotional data through My Account. Their shared CrownMind login remains available. GCash transaction and review records are retained separately. For help, email contact@crownmind.net.

Children

Children should use the service with a parent or guardian. The service is not designed to solicit personal data from children.

Security

We use access restrictions and HTTPS when deployed. No system can guarantee complete security. Never place payment information, passwords or identifying secrets inside prayer intentions.

Changes

Policy updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

This is an independent project, not an official Basilica or Archdiocese application.
