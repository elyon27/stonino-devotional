Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

The Sto. Niño Devotional App is an independent devotional service operated by Bienuel C. Tenio. Contact: contact@crownmind.net.

Guest use

Anyone can access prayers without creating an account. Candles, intentions, completion progress, language preferences and text-size preferences are kept in browser storage on the user's device. Guest prayer intentions stay in your browser. Clearing browser storage or using a different device may erase them.

Direct GCash checkout

GCash processes the ₱1 transfer. The operator compares your submitted transaction reference with the transfer received in GCash. The checkout stores a digest of the reference, an order identifier, the offering type and the review status. Prayer notes stay out of the payment request. Keep the checkout in the same browser until your offering appears.

Optional accounts

When cloud service is activated, the app uses Supabase authentication for email and password management. The service stores account identifiers, private prayer intentions, candle metadata and novena progress in a database restricted by per-user access rules. The account provider manages authentication data. Do not enter highly sensitive information you do not wish to store.

Use and sharing

Information is used only to display and synchronize requested devotional features. The initial release does not include a public prayer wall, advertising, paid spiritual services or sale of personal data. Operational hosting providers may process technical service data according to their own policies.

Retention and choices

Guest data remains on your browser until deleted or cleared. Cloud data is kept until account deletion or a lawful retention requirement. You may export or delete guest data under Settings. Cloud account users can delete the account within the app when cloud service is configured and available. For help, email contact@crownmind.net.

Children

Children should use the service with a parent or guardian. The service is not designed to solicit personal data from children.

Security

We use access restrictions and HTTPS when deployed. No system can guarantee complete security. Never place payment information, passwords or identifying secrets inside prayer intentions.

Changes

Policy updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

This is an independent project, not an official Basilica or Archdiocese application.
