Delete a cloud account

  1. Open the app and sign in to your account.
  2. Select My Account in the top navigation.
  3. Choose Delete account and cloud data.
  4. Confirm the permanent deletion request.

The deletion feature becomes active when the application's Supabase account service and secure Netlify deletion endpoint have been configured by the operator. If the feature is not available, email contact@crownmind.net with the subject “Sto. Niño Account Deletion” from the account's registered email address. Do not send your password.

What is deleted?

The account's authentication record, private prayer intentions, active candle data and novena progress are deleted. An optional exported JSON backup on your own device is not deleted remotely. Service-level security logs may be retained by infrastructure providers in accordance with applicable law and retention requirements.

Guest data

If you never registered, navigate to Settings & Privacy → Delete all guest data, or clear the site's browser storage. Guest data does not reside in a cloud account.

Account deletion is free. Contact support for questions or assistance.
