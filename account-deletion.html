Delete your account

  1. Open the app and sign in to your account.
  2. Select My Account in the top navigation.
  3. Choose Delete My Account.
  4. Confirm the permanent deletion request.

The deletion feature becomes active when the app is connected to Supabase. If the feature is not available, email contact@crownmind.net with the subject “Sto. Niño Data Deletion” from the account's registered email address. Do not send your password.

What is deleted?

Private prayer intentions, candles, flowers, novena progress and your remaining Prayer Pack balance are removed from the cloud and this browser. Your shared CrownMind sign-in and access to other apps are preserved. Payment and legacy review records are retained separately. Prayer Pack purchase records are detached from your profile. An optional exported JSON backup on your own device is not deleted remotely. Service-level security logs may be retained by infrastructure providers in accordance with applicable law and retention requirements.

Data deletion is free. Contact support for questions or assistance.
