    Fiesta Señor Nine-Day Novena

    Basilica Minore del Santo Niño de Cebu, 2019 Pilgrim’s Booklet, pp. 3–9

    Act of Contrition

    Oh, Dear Jesus, You are our King and our God, because of your love for us, You came down from heaven and born of the Virgin Mary. We repent all the transgressions we have committed against you. Forgive us, Oh Jesus, for our sins of ingratitude. Strengthen our will to resist temptations. Grant us the grace not to sin again that we may enjoy the blessings of Heaven.

    Oh, Holy Child Jesus, son of the living God, who is ever-good and loving. We kneel before you our True God. We humbly ask to give us your mercy and grant us all the favors we ask of You in this Novena.

    AMEN.

    Pray 3 Our Father; 3 Hail Mary & 3 Glory be…

    Now ask Senor Santo Nino your intentions for making this Novena.

    Supplications to Santo Niño

    Lord, have mercy on us.

    In every need let us come to you with humble trust.

    R. Santo Nino, help us.

    In hours of loneliness, weariness and trials,
    R. Santo Nino, help us.

    In the failure of our plans and hopes.
    R. Santo Nino, help us.

    In disappointments, troubles and sorrows,
    R. Santo Nino, help us.

    When others fail us, and Your Grace alone can assist us.
    R. Santo Nino, help us.

    When we throw ourselves on your tender love as our only refuge.
    R. Santo Nino, help us.

    When we are ill and our head and hands cannot work and we are lonely.
    R. Santo Nino, help us.

    When we feel impatient and our cross irritates us.
    R. Santo Nino, help us.

    Always, always, in spite of weaknesses, failings and shortcomings of every kind.
    R. Santo Nino, help us.

    Lamb of God, who take away the sins of the world:

    Have mercy upon us, O Jesus.

    Jesus, hear us.

    Reflection

    Jesus calls us to receive the Kingdom with a childlike heart. Humility makes room for grace; trust turns us toward our heavenly Father.

    Prayer

    Lord Jesus, Holy Child, teach me to trust you with an open heart. Help me release pride and fear, and follow you in humility. May I become attentive to your will today. Amen.
